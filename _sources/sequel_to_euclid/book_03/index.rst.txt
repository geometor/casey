Book III: Additional Propositions & Exercises
=============================================

.. rubric:: Additional Propositions & Exercises Extending Euclid's Book III (pp. 29–52, Folios 0055–0078)

Book III of John Casey's *Sequel to Euclid* (5th ed., 1888) establishes the modern theory of the circle: inverse points, circle power, orthogonal circles, the radical axis, the radical centre, Poncelet's limiting points, and the foundational projective theory of poles and polars (:person:`La Hire <la-hire>`).
