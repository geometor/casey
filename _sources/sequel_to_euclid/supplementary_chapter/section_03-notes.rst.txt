Supplementary Chapter, Section III Notes: Lemoine, Tucker & Taylor
==================================================================

.. rubric:: Discovery History of Six-Point Triangle Circles (1873–1883)

.. folio:: 0205
   :page: 179

1. Lemoine (1873) & Tucker's "Triplicate-Ratio Circle" (1883)
-------------------------------------------------------------

:person:`Émile Lemoine <lemoine>` discovered both the First Lemoine Circle and the Cosine Circle in his 1873 Lyon Congress address (:cite:`*Note sur un point remarquable* <lemoine:1873>`). Ten years later, :person:`Robert Tucker <tucker>` independently rediscovered the First Circle in :cite:`QJM 19 (1883) <tucker:1883>` under the name **Triplicate-Ratio Circle** (highlighting the $a^3 : b^3 : c^3$ side-chord law) before realizing both circles sit inside a continuous one-parameter family centered on the Brocard diameter $OK$.

.. folio:: 0210
   :page: 184

2. H. M. Taylor's Six-Point Circle & French Precedents
------------------------------------------------------

On f. ``0210`` (p. 184), Casey attributes the six-point projection circle of the orthic triangle to H. M. Taylor (*Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society*, vol. XV, p. 122), noting its immediate integration into the Tucker system—indexed in ``geometor/etc`` via $X(182)$ for the First Lemoine centre and ``geometor/altshiller-court`` §§ 616–628.
