1. The One-Parameter Tucker Pencil of Six-Point Circles
-------------------------------------------------------

Section III unifies the classical six-point triangle circles as specializations of **Tucker's family**, generated by intersecting $\triangle ABC$ with a homothetic triangle $\triangle A'B'C'$ centered at the Symmedian Point $K = X(6)$:

* **Lemoine's First (Triplicate-Ratio) Circle (`(Ml D1)` in `0205.g1.svg`):** Cut by the three **parallels** to the sides through $K$; centered at $M_L = X(182) = \operatorname{midpoint}(O, K)$ with side chords $\frac{a^3}{a^2+b^2+c^2}, \frac{b^3}{a^2+b^2+c^2}, \frac{c^3}{a^2+b^2+c^2}$ in triplicate ratio $a^3 : b^3 : c^3$.
* **Cosine (Second Lemoine) Circle (`(K Cb2)` in `0205.g1.svg`):** Cut by the three **antiparallels** to the sides through $K$; centered at $K$ itself with radius $R\tan\omega = \frac{abc}{a^2+b^2+c^2}$ and side chords proportional to $\cos A : \cos B : \cos C$.
* **Taylor's Circle (f. ``0210``):** The six perpendicular projections of the orthic triangle's feet upon the other two sides of $\triangle ABC$ form a Tucker circle centered at the incentre of the medial triangle of the orthic triangle.
