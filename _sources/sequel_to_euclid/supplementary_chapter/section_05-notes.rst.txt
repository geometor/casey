Supplementary Chapter, Section V Notes: Brocard, Neuberg, M'Cay & Feuerbach
===========================================================================

.. rubric:: Historical Provenance & Cross-Canon Concordance (`etc`, `altshiller-court`)

.. folio:: 0220
   :page: 194

1. Brocard's Circle in Algiers & Rouen (1876–1883)
--------------------------------------------------

On f. ``0221`` (p. 195), Casey explicitly credits Captain H. Brocard (*Chef de Bataillon*), noting his papers in *Nouvelle Correspondance Mathématique*, tomes III–VI (1876–1879), and his addresses before the *Association Française pour l'Avancement des Sciences* at the Algiers (1881, :cite:`AFAS Alger, 1881 <brocard:1881>`) and Rouen (1883) Congresses, subsequently expanded by :person:`Neuberg <neuberg>`, M'Cay, and :person:`Tucker <tucker>`.

.. folio:: 0228
   :page: 202

2. Multiple Independent Proofs of Feuerbach's Theorem in Casey's *Sequel*
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Together with the two inversion/Casey-theorem proofs in Book VI, Section IV (ff. ``0130–0132``), Section V (ff. ``0227–0229``) supplies a completely independent proof of :person:`Feuerbach's <feuerbach>` Nine-Points Tangency Theorem from the **Circle of Similitude** of the three half-scale triangles $AB'C', A'BC', A'B'C$ (further enriched by :person:`W. J. M'Clelland <mclelland>` acknowledged in Casey's Preface, f. ``0008``).
