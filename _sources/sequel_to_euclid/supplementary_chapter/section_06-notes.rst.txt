Supplementary Chapter, Section VI Notes: Neuberg (1885) & Casey (1886)
======================================================================

.. rubric:: Casey's Original 1886 Royal Irish Academy Memoir in Textbook Form

.. folio:: 0231
   :page: 205

1. From Neuberg's Harmonic Quadrilaterals to Casey's $n$-Gons
-------------------------------------------------------------

After :person:`Joseph Neuberg <neuberg>` introduced *harmonic quadrilaterals* ($AB\cdot CD = BC\cdot DA$) in :cite:`*Mathesis* 5 (1885) <neuberg:1885>`, :person:`John Casey <casey>` immediately generalized the entire Lemoine–Brocard–Tucker apparatus to arbitrary cyclic **Harmonic $n$-gons** in his landmark memoir *On the Harmonic Section of Polygons* (*Proceedings of the Royal Irish Academy*, :cite:`1886 <casey:1886:harmonic>`).

.. folio:: 0236
   :page: 210

Casey incorporated his Academy memoir directly into Section VI (ff. ``0231–0248``, pp. 205–222) of the 1886 and 1888 editions, creating one of the earliest English textbook expositions bridging projective involution on a circle with classical polygon geometry (see also :person:`Altshiller-Court <altshiller-court>` and ``geometor/pappus``).
