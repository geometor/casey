Unit 6 Notes: Brocard, Neuberg, Casey's Harmonic Polygons & Kimberling's ETC
============================================================================

.. rubric:: International Collaboration Behind the 1888–1892 Supplementary Chapter

1. Casey's Transnational Network (Brocard, Neuberg, Tucker, M'Clelland)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

In the Preface to the Fifth Edition (f. ``0008``, p. vi), :person:`John Casey <casey>` explicitly acknowledges his personal correspondence with :person:`Joseph Neuberg <neuberg>` (Liège), :person:`Henri Brocard <brocard>` (Montpellier/Algiers), :person:`Robert Tucker <tucker>` (London), and :person:`W. J. M'Clelland <mclelland>` (Dublin). Notice two historical landmarks unique to Unit 6:

* **Brocard's 1881 Algiers Memoir (:cite:`AFAS Alger, 1881 <brocard:1881>`):** Although Crelle (1816) and Jacobi (1825) had briefly studied the equi-angle points $\Omega, \Omega'$, Brocard discovered the **Seven-Point Brocard Circle** on diameter $[OK]$ unifying $O, K, \Omega, \Omega'$ and $\triangle A_1 B_1 C_1$.
* **Harmonic Polygons (Casey & Neuberg, 1885–1886):** After :person:`Neuberg <neuberg>` introduced *harmonic quadrilaterals* ($AB\cdot CD = BC\cdot DA$, :cite:`*Mathesis* 5, 1885 <neuberg:1885>`), Casey immediately generalized the entire Lemoine–Brocard–Tucker apparatus to arbitrary cyclic **Harmonic $n$-gons** in the *Proceedings of the Royal Irish Academy* (:cite:`1886 <casey:1886:harmonic>`) and incorporated that memoir directly as Section VI of the Supplementary Chapter (pp. 205–218).

2. Cross-Canon Links (`geometor/etc` & `geometor/altshiller-court`)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Every special point in Unit 6 maps directly into ``geometor/etc`` (:cite:`Kimberling's *ETC* <kimberling:etc>`) and ``geometor/altshiller-court`` (:cite:`*College Geometry*, Ch. X, §§ 629–689 <altshiller-court:1952>`):

* ``X(3)`` Circumcentre $O$ & ``X(6)`` Symmedian Point $K$ (diameter of Brocard Circle);
* ``X(20)`` de Longchamps Point $L = 2O - H$ (Casey Misc. Ex. 146–158, p. 247);
* ``X(39)`` Brocard Midpoint $\frac{1}{2}(\Omega + \Omega')$ on $[OK]$;
* ``X(98)`` Tarry Point $T_r$ & ``X(99)`` Steiner Point $S_t$ (antipodal pair on the circumcircle);
* ``X(182)`` Midpoint $M_L = \frac{1}{2}(O + K)$, common centre of Lemoine's First Circle and the Brocard Circle;
* Bicentric pair $(\Omega, \Omega')$: note that because $\Omega$ and $\Omega'$ permute cyclically rather than symmetrically under swap of $B \leftrightarrow C$, they form the **prototype bicentric pair** (rather than a single symmetric Kimberling triangle center), while their midpoint ``X(39)`` and line ``ΩΩ'`` are fully symmetric!
