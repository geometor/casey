geometor.casey.coaxial¶

Exact straightedge-and-compass constructions for Radical Axes, Coaxal Systems & Limiting Points.

Implements Unit 2 of the Casey Codex:

  • Book III, Prop. 22 (p. 42): Orthogonal circles (|O1 O2|^2 = r1^2 + r2^2).

  • Book III, Prop. 23 & Defs. (p. 43): Radical Axis, Radical Centre, Radical Circle, and Poncelet’s Limiting Points L1, L2 on the line of centres (|D L1| = |D L2| = sqrt(pow)).

  • Book III, Prop. 24 (p. 44): Fundamental Theorem of Coaxal Circles: the difference of the squares of tangents from any point P to two circles equals twice the rectangle contained by the distance between their centres and the perpendicular from P to their radical axis: pow(P, C1) - pow(P, C2) = 2 * (O2 - O1) . (E - P).

  • Book III, Prop. 28 & Book VI, Sec. V (pp. 113–119): Compass-and-straightedge construction of the radical axis of two disjoint circles via an auxiliary secant circle and conjugate orthogonal pencils passing through L1 and L2.

geometor.casey.coaxial.construct_radical_axis_and_coaxial_pencil(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → CoaxialPencilResult¶

Construct the radical axis, coaxal system, orthogonal pencil, and limiting points.

Uses exact integer-rational circles C1 = ((-10, 0), 6) and C2 = ((17, 0), 15):

  1. Draws an auxiliary secant circle C_aux = ((2, 8), 10) cutting C1 in U1, V1 and C2 in U2, V2 (Casey Book III, Prop. 28).

  2. Intersects common chords [U1 V1] and [U2 V2] to obtain the Radical Centre Rc = (0, -6) (Book III, Prop. 23, Cor. 2).

  3. Drops the perpendicular from Rc to the line of centres [O1 O2] to construct the Radical Axis x = 0 and foot D = (0, 0).

  4. Constructs the tangent D T1 to C1 by Euclid III.17 (Thales circle on O1 D) and transfers |D T1| = 8 onto [O1 O2] via circle (D, T1) to construct Poncelet’s Limiting Points L1 = (-8, 0) and L2 = (8, 0).

  5. Constructs two members (Rc, L1) and (D, T1) of the conjugate orthogonal pencil passing through L1, L2 and cutting C1, C2, C3 orthogonally.

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated CoaxialPencilResult with exact coordinates and verification report.
