Book II: Additional Propositions & Exercises
============================================

.. rubric:: Additional Propositions & Exercises Extending Euclid's Book II (pp. 21–28, Folios 0047–0054)

Book II of John Casey's *Sequel to Euclid* (5th ed., 1888) extends geometric algebra to quadratic segment identities, median theorems (:person:`Apollonius <apollonius>`, :person:`Stewart <stewart>`), quadrilateral midpoint theorems (:person:`Euler <euler>`), and the general quadratic moment theorem for centres of mean position (:person:`Leibniz <leibniz>`).
