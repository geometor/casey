Book IV: Additional Propositions & Exercises
=============================================

.. rubric:: Additional Propositions & Exercises Extending Euclid's Book IV (pp. 53–66, Folios 0079–0092)

Book IV of John Casey's *Sequel to Euclid* (5th ed., 1888) treats inscribed, circumscribed, and escribed circles of triangles and regular polygons. It establishes fundamental relations between inradii and exradii, Heron's formula for triangle area, metric properties of regular polygons, the Nine-Points Circle (:person:`Euler <euler>`, :person:`Feuerbach <feuerbach>`), the tangency of the Nine-Points Circle to the incircle and excircles (Feuerbach's Theorem), circumcircle bisector projections, and mean centres of vertices.
