1. Ancient Roots in Pappus Book VII (`geometor/pappus`)
-------------------------------------------------------

The harmonic properties of collinear segments, pencils, and the complete quadrilateral descend directly from :person:`Pappus of Alexandria's <pappus>` *Synagoge* Book VII (c. 340 CE, modeled symbolically in ``geometor/pappus``):

* **Harmonic Cross-Ratio Invariance:** *Synagoge* VII, Prop. 129 proves that any transversal cutting a pencil of four concurrent lines with harmonic ratio is cut in a harmonic range.
* **Complete Quadrilateral Diagonals:** *Synagoge* VII, Prop. 131 contains the foundational theorem that each diagonal of a complete quadrilateral is divided harmonically by the other two diagonals.
