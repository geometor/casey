1. Joseph Neuberg & William S. M'Cay (1884–1887)
------------------------------------------------

In the 1880s, :person:`Joseph Neuberg <neuberg>` (Liège, *Mémoire sur le tétraèdre* and *Mathesis*) and William S. M'Cay (Trinity College Dublin, *Transactions of the Royal Irish Academy*) elevated Brocard geometry from ad-hoc triangle identities into a general Euclidean transformation theory for **systems of three directly similar figures**. :person:`John Casey <casey>` systematically unified their results across Section IV (ff. ``0215–0220``) so that the Brocard Circle, M'Cay circles, and Feuerbach's theorem fall out as single-step specializations.
