Supplementary Chapter, Section VII Notes: Neuberg, Tarry, Simmons & M'Cay
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Archival Priority & 1886–1888 Bibliographical Notes

.. folio:: 0249
   :page: 223

1. Priority Note on Harmonic Polygons (March 1886 – 5th Ed. 1888)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

On f. ``0249`` (p. 223), Casey prints a 1886 remark by Prof. W. S. M'Cay (Trinity College Dublin, appended to Tucker's paper) anticipating harmonic-polygon extensions, and credits MM. :person:`Joseph Neuberg <neuberg>` and :person:`Gaston Tarry <tarry>` (*AFAS Nancy*, 1886) for Prop. 2 and Rev. T. C. Simmons (*Proc. LMS*, April 1887) for the enunciation of Prop. 7, claiming independent original priority for the remaining propositions of Section VII.

.. folio:: 0251
   :page: 225

2. Naming of Tarry's Line & Citation of *Mathesis* VI
-----------------------------------------------------

In his footnote spanning ff. ``0251–0252`` (pp. 225–226), Casey explicitly names *Tarry's Line* and *Tarry's Polygon* after *"M. Gaston Tarry, 'Receveur des contributions, à Alger,' to whom the theory of associated figures is due. See Mathesis, Tome vi., pp. 97, 148, 196."*
