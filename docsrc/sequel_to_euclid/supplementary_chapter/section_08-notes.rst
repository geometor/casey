Supplementary Chapter, Section VIII Notes: Neuberg, M'Cay & de Longchamps
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Historical Provenance & Folio Colophon (ff. 0257–0274, pp. 231–248)

.. folio:: 0257
   :page: 231

1. Continental & Irish Collaborators Across Exercises 1–145
-----------------------------------------------------------

Across the 158 exercises of Section VIII, Casey draws extensively from :person:`Joseph Neuberg <neuberg>` (on Neuberg circles and co-symmedian triangles), Prof. W. S. M'Cay (co-symmedian perspective lines, Ex. 13), :person:`Émile Lemoine <lemoine>`, and :person:`Gaston Tarry <tarry>`, bridging Irish, British, French, and Belgian periodicals (*Mathesis*, *Messenger of Mathematics*, *Educational Times*) into a unified problem set.

.. folio:: 0273
   :page: 247

2. :person:`Gaston Albert Gohierre de Longchamps <longchamps>` (Exs. 146–158)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

On f. ``0273`` (p. 247), Casey acknowledges M. :person:`de Longchamps <longchamps>` (*Journal de Mathématiques Spéciales*, 1886; note the printed misprint *"1866"* on line 4 of p. 247) for the orthogonal-median-circle theorem defining $L = 2O - H = X(20)$, concluding the 1888 Dublin Fifth Edition on f. ``0274`` (p. 248), followed only by the publisher's endpapers.
