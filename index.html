semantic digital archive • the works of

John Casey¶

The mission of this project is to capture a semantic digital library of the works of John Casey (1820–1891), Fellow of the Royal University of Ireland and author of foundational treatises extending Euclidean geometry into modern synthetic circle, projective, and triangle geometry. Within the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive, this work is liberated from static print bounds—decomposed into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and cross-corpus citations speaking one shared geometric language.

About

geometor/casey establishes the authoritative Digital Scriptorium edition and exact symbolic engine for John Casey’s landmark nineteenth-century treatise, A Sequel to the First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid (1888 / 1892). Casey (1820–1891), Fellow of the Royal Society and Professor of Higher Mathematics at the Catholic University of Ireland, stood alongside George Salmon, Richard Townsend, and Robert Stawell Ball at the summit of the nineteenth-century Dublin school of pure geometry.
