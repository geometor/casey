geometor.casey.brocard¶

Exact symbolic constructions and proofs for Symmedians, Lemoine Circles & Brocard Geometry.

Implements Units 5 & 6 of the Casey Codex (Supplementary Chapter, Sections I–VII, pp. 165–248):

  • Symmedians & Lemoine’s Point ``K = X(6)`` (Sec. I, Props. 5–7, pp. 170–172): Constructed via circumcircle tangents (Prop. 6), verifying barycentric weights (a^2 : b^2 : c^2) and Lagrange’s minimum-sum-of-squares property min(x^2 + y^2 + z^2) = 4*Delta^2 / (a^2+b^2+c^2).

  • First Lemoine Circle & Cosine Circle (Sec. III, Prop. 1 & Cor. 4, pp. 179–183): Six concyclic side-parallel intersections D1, D2, E1, E2, F1, F2 centered at Ml = midpoint(O, K) with intercepts a^3 : b^3 : c^3, and six concyclic antiparallel intersections centered at K.

  • Brocard Points ``Om1, Om2``, Brocard Angle ``omega``, Brocard’s First Triangle & Brocard Circle (Sec. II, pp. 175–179; Sec. V, pp. 194–196): Constructed by pure compass-and-straightedge tangent circles, verifying cot(omega) = cot A + cot B + cot C = (a^2 + b^2 + c^2) / (4*Delta) and the seven-point Brocard Circle on diameter OK containing O, K, Om1, Om2, A1, B1, C1.

  • Associated Figures: Steiner Point ``X(99)``, Tarry Point ``X(98)`` & Longchamps Point ``X(20)`` (Sec. VII & Misc. Ex. 146–158, p. 247).

geometor.casey.brocard.verify_brocard_identities_symbolic() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Verify Casey’s universal algebraic identities for cot(omega) and K’s minimum property.

  1. By the Law of Cosines, cot A + cot B + cot C = (b^2+c^2-a^2)/(4*Delta) + (c^2+a^2-b^2)/(4*Delta) + (a^2+b^2-c^2)/(4*Delta) = (a^2+b^2+c^2)/(4*Delta).

  2. By Lagrange’s identity (Sec. I, Prop. 7, p. 171): (a^2+b^2+c^2)*(x^2+y^2+z^2) - (a*x+b*y+c*z)^2 = (b*z - c*y)^2 + (c*x - a*z)^2 + (a*y - b*x)^2, proving that under a*x+b*y+c*z = 2*Delta, x^2 + y^2 + z^2 attains its unique minimum when x/a = y/b = z/c (at K = X(6)).

Returns:

Dictionary of zero-verified symbolic residuals.

geometor.casey.brocard.construct_brocard_and_lemoine_circles(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → BrocardLemoineResult¶

Construct the Brocard Points, Brocard Circle, First Lemoine Circle, Steiner & Tarry Points.

On the Heronian (15, 13, 14) triangle A=(0,0), B=(14,0), C=(5,12):

  1. Constructs O = (7, 33/8), K = X(6) = (1673/295, 1176/295), and the First Lemoine Circle centered at Ml = midpoint(O, K) = (4053/590, 9567/2360).

  2. Constructs the First Brocard Point ``Om1`` via tangent circles (Ob1, B) (tangent to BC at B through A) and (Oc1, C) (tangent to CA at C through B), verifying concurrency with (Oa1, A) (tangent to AB at A through C).

  3. Constructs the Second Brocard Point ``Om2`` via reversed tangent circles.

  4. Constructs Brocard’s First Triangle ``A1 B1 C1`` (A1 = [B Om1] ∩ [C Om2], etc.) and proves that all seven points ``O, K, Om1, Om2, A1, B1, C1`` lie on the Brocard Circle with diameter OK (Casey, Supp. Sec. V, pp. 194–195).

  5. Constructs Steiner’s Point ``St = X(99)`` (parallels through A, B, C to sides of A1 B1 C1), Tarry’s Point ``Tr = X(98)`` (antipode of St on circumcircle), and de Longchamps’ Point ``Lo = X(20)`` (reflection of H across O).

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated BrocardLemoineResult with full exact symbolic report.
