geometor.casey.concordance¶

Cross-repository concordance and SymPy capability audit for Casey’s Sequel to Euclid.

Provides structured access to resources/sequel_to_euclid/concordance.json mapping Casey’s propositions across euclid, elements, altshiller-court, etc, and pappus, alongside an empirical audit of sympy.geometry.Circle capability gaps filled by geometor.casey.
