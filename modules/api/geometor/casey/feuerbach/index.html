geometor.casey.feuerbach¶

Exact symbolic proofs and compass-and-straightedge constructions of Casey’s & Feuerbach’s Theorems.

Implements:

  • Unit 1 (Book I Misc. Ex. 42–46 & Book IV Prop. 5, pp. 20, 57–58): The Euler line (O, G, N, H) and the Nine-Points Circle through side midpoints Ma, Mb, Mc, altitude feet Da, Db, Dc, and Euler midpoints Ea, Eb, Ec.

  • Unit 4 (Book VI, Sec. IV, Prop. 9, Prop. 10 & Cor. 2, pp. 102–106):

    1. Universal polynomial proof of Casey’s Generalized Ptolemy Theorem (t_12*t_34 + t_14*t_23 = t_13*t_24) via circular inversion and bitangent invariance.

    2. Universal side-length (a, b, c) bitangent proof of Feuerbach’s Theorem for both the incircle and the excircles.

    3. Exact compass-and-straightedge construction on the canonical Heronian (15, 13, 14) triangle verifying bitangent lengths, Casey’s 1861 orthogonal inversion circle at Ma, and exact tangency at the Feuerbach point Fe = X(11) = (6, 8) plus all three excircles.

geometor.casey.feuerbach.prove_casey_theorem_symbolic() → CaseySymbolicProof¶

Prove Casey’s Generalized Ptolemy Theorem & Feuerbach’s Corollary symbolically.

Verifies four algebraic pillars from Casey, Book VI, Sec. IV, Props. 9–10 (pp. 102–106):

  1. Inversion Invariance (Prop. 9, pp. 102–103): Under inversion in (O, k) at the origin, for two circles (x1, y1, r1) and (x2, y2, r2) not passing through O, the normalized bitangent ratio t_12^2 / (r1 * r2) is strictly invariant: t'_12^2 / (r'_1 * r'_2) - t_12^2 / (r1 * r2) == 0.

  2. Collinear Contact Reduction (Prop. 10, pp. 103–104): Inverting from a point on the fifth tangent circle maps it to y = 0 and the four tangent circles to (u_i, r_i) with t_ij = |u_i - u_j|, reducing t_12*t_34 + t_14*t_23 = t_13*t_24 for ordered contacts u_1 < u_2 < u_3 < u_4 to Euler’s identity (Book II, Prop. 7, p. 24).

  3. Universal Incircle Bitangent Identity (Prop. 10, Cor. 2, pp. 104–105): For general positive side lengths b <= c <= a, t_23 = a/2, t_31 = b/2, t_12 = c/2 and t_14 = (c - b)/2, t_24 = (a - c)/2, t_34 = (a - b)/2 satisfy t_23*t_14 + t_31*t_24 - t_12*t_34 == 0, alongside Casey’s squared identity (b^2 - c^2) + (c^2 - a^2) + (a^2 - b^2) == 0.

  4. Universal Excircle Bitangent Identity: For the A-excircle, t_14 = (c - b)/2, t_24 = (a + c)/2, t_34 = (a + b)/2 satisfy t_23*t_14 + t_31*t_24 - t_12*t_34 == 0 (with t_31*t_24 = t_12*t_34 + t_23*t_14).

Returns:

Frozen CaseySymbolicProof certificate with zero residuals.

geometor.casey.feuerbach.prove_casey_feuerbach_heronian() → CaseyFeuerbachHeronianReport¶

Verify Casey’s bitangent equation and all 4 Feuerbach tangencies on Heronian (15, 13, 14).

Evaluates exact coordinates on A=(0,0), B=(14,0), C=(5,12) (a=15, b=13, c=14), confirming:

  • Midpoint chords t_23 = 15/2, t_31 = 13/2, t_12 = 7.

  • Midpoint-to-incircle bitangents |Ma Ta| = 1/2, |Mb Tb| = 1/2, |Mc Tc| = 1.

  • Casey’s equation (15/2)*(1/2) + (13/2)*(1/2) - 7*1 = 15/4 + 13/4 - 7 = 0.

  • Internal tangency of Nine-Points Circle N=(6, 63/16), R_9=65/16 with Incircle I=(6, 4), r=4 at Fe = X(11) = (6, 8) with |NI| = 1/16 = R_9 - r.

  • External tangency of Nine-Points Circle with all three Excircles (Ia, r_a), (Ib, r_b), (Ic, r_c) with |N I_k| = R_9 + r_k.

  • Casey’s 1861 inversion circle (Ma, 1/2) cuts both (I, 4) and (Ia, 14) orthogonally and inverts their fourth common internal tangent into the Nine-Points Circle.

Returns:

Frozen CaseyFeuerbachHeronianReport with exact rational values.

geometor.casey.feuerbach.construct_casey_feuerbach(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → CaseyFeuerbachResult¶

Construct Casey’s Inversion Proof & Feuerbach’s Tangency Theorem on (15, 13, 14).

Builds the Nine-Points Circle, constructs the Incentre I and Incircle (I, Ta) by pure compass-and-straightedge (Euclid IV.4), erects Casey’s 1861 orthogonal inversion circle (Ma, Ta) (Book VI, Sec. IV, p. 106), constructs the fourth common internal tangent of (I, r) and (Ia, r_a) via reflection across line of centres AI, and discovers the exact Feuerbach point Fe = X(11) = (6, 8) as (N, Ma) ∩ (I, Ta).

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Populated CaseyFeuerbachResult.
