geometor.casey.poles_polars¶

Exact straightedge-and-compass constructions for Harmonic Section, Poles, Polars & Reciprocity.

Implements:

  • Unit 3 (Book VI, Sec. III, Prop. 7, p. 91): Pure-straightedge Complete Quadrilateral harmonic ranges (A, C; M, N) = -1.

  • Unit 4 (Book III, Def. & Props. 25, 27, pp. 44–47; Book VI, Sec. III, Prop. 4, p. 89; Book VI, Sec. VII, Props. 1–6, pp. 141–147):

    1. Universal symbolic proof of La Hire’s Reciprocity Theorem (Prop. 25): Q in polar(P) <=> P in polar(Q).

    2. Pure-straightedge construction of polar(D) via an inscribed complete quadrangle G1 H1 H2 G2 on x^2 + y^2 = 25, producing the Self-Conjugate Triangle D U V (Sec. VII, Prop. 2, p. 142).

    3. Verification of Harmonic Secant Section (G1, H1; J1, D) = -1 and (G2, H2; J2, D) = -1 (Sec. III, Prop. 4, p. 89).

    4. Compass-and-straightedge chord-of-contact construction of polar(Q) for Q = (4, -4) lying on polar(D), confirming D in polar(Q) (Book III, Props. 25 & 27).

geometor.casey.poles_polars.verify_lahire_reciprocity_symbolic() → LaHireSymbolicProof¶

Prove La Hire’s Reciprocity (Book III, Prop. 25) and Harmonic Secant Section (VI.III.4).

  1. For circle (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2, polar(P) has equation (x1 - h)(x - h) + (y1 - k)(y - k) - r^2 = 0, which is symmetric in (x1, y1) and (x2, y2).

  2. For exterior pole D = (d, 0) on diameter secant y = 0 cutting x^2 + y^2 = r^2 at G = (-r, 0), H = (r, 0) with polar foot J = (r^2/d, 0), the anharmonic ratio (G, H; J, D) simplifies identically to -1.

Returns:

Frozen LaHireSymbolicProof certificate.

geometor.casey.poles_polars.construct_complete_quadrilateral_harmonic(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → CompleteQuadrilateralResult¶

Construct Casey’s Complete Quadrilateral and verify harmonic diagonal division (Unit 3).

Follows Casey, Book VI, Sec. III, Prop. 7 (p. 91) using pure straightedge incidences on exact integer vertices A=(0, 0), B=(6, 0), C=(4, 2), D=(2, 3):

  • Opposite side meets: E = [AB] ∩ [CD] = (8, 0) and F = [AD] ∩ [BC] = (12/5, 18/5).

  • Internal diagonal meet: P = [AC] ∩ [BD] = (18/5, 9/5).

  • Third diagonal [EF] cuts [AC] at Qa = (9/2, 9/4) and [BD] at Qb = (-6, 9) such that (A, C; P, Qa) = -1 and (B, D; P, Qb) = -1.

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated CompleteQuadrilateralResult.

geometor.casey.poles_polars.construct_pole_and_polar(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → PolePolarResult¶

Construct the Polar of D by straightedge and verify La Hire’s Reciprocity (Unit 4).

On circle (O, R0) with O = (0, 0) and R0 = (5, 0) (x^2 + y^2 = 25):

  1. Draws two rational secants cutting the circle in G1=(0, 5), H1=(4, 3) and G2=(-3, -4), H2=(5, 0), meeting at exterior pole D = (15/2, 5/4).

  2. Constructs the remaining diagonal vertices U = [G1 H2] ∩ [G2 H1] = (3, 2) and V = [G1 G2] ∩ [H1 H2] = (5/3, 10) of the inscribed quadrangle; their join [U V] is polar(D): 6x + y - 20 = 0, and D U V is a Self-Conjugate Triangle (Casey, Book VI, Sec. VII, Prop. 2, p. 142).

  3. Intersects polar(D) with both secants at J1, J2 and verifies Casey’s Harmonic Secant Theorem (G1, H1; J1, D) = (G2, H2; J2, D) = -1 (Sec. III, Prop. 4, p. 89).

  4. Selects exterior point Q = (4, -4) on polar(D), constructs its two tangents to (O, 5) by Thales semicircle on OQ, draws chord of contact polar(Q) (Book III, Prop. 27, p. 47), and asserts La Hire’s Reciprocity D in polar(Q) (Book III, Prop. 25, p. 45).

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated PolePolarResult.
