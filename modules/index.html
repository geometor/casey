geometor.casey¶

Exact symbolic geometry constructions and Digital Scriptorium apparatus for John Casey’s Sequel.

Models the landmark theorems and straightedge-and-compass constructions of John Casey’s A Sequel to the First Six Books of the Elements of Euclid, Containing an Easy Introduction to Modern Geometry (5th ed. 1888 / 6th ed. 1892):

  • Euler Line & Nine-Points Circle (construct_euler_nine_point())

  • Radical Axis, Coaxal Circles & Poncelet Limiting Points (construct_radical_axis_and_coaxial_pencil(), verify_coaxial_fundamental_theorem())

  • Complete Quadrilateral Harmonic Division (construct_complete_quadrilateral_harmonic())

  • Casey’s Generalized Ptolemy Theorem & Feuerbach’s Theorem (prove_casey_theorem_symbolic(), prove_casey_feuerbach_heronian(), construct_casey_feuerbach())

  • Poles, Polars, Harmonic Secants & La Hire’s Reciprocity (construct_pole_and_polar(), verify_lahire_reciprocity_symbolic())

  • Symmedians, Lemoine Circles, Brocard Points, Brocard Circle, Steiner & Tarry Points (construct_symmedian_and_lemoine_circles(), construct_brocard_and_lemoine_circles())

geometor.casey.construct_brocard_and_lemoine_circles(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → BrocardLemoineResult¶

Construct the Brocard Points, Brocard Circle, First Lemoine Circle, Steiner & Tarry Points.

On the Heronian (15, 13, 14) triangle A=(0,0), B=(14,0), C=(5,12):

  1. Constructs O = (7, 33/8), K = X(6) = (1673/295, 1176/295), and the First Lemoine Circle centered at Ml = midpoint(O, K) = (4053/590, 9567/2360).

  2. Constructs the First Brocard Point ``Om1`` via tangent circles (Ob1, B) (tangent to BC at B through A) and (Oc1, C) (tangent to CA at C through B), verifying concurrency with (Oa1, A) (tangent to AB at A through C).

  3. Constructs the Second Brocard Point ``Om2`` via reversed tangent circles.

  4. Constructs Brocard’s First Triangle ``A1 B1 C1`` (A1 = [B Om1] ∩ [C Om2], etc.) and proves that all seven points ``O, K, Om1, Om2, A1, B1, C1`` lie on the Brocard Circle with diameter OK (Casey, Supp. Sec. V, pp. 194–195).

  5. Constructs Steiner’s Point ``St = X(99)`` (parallels through A, B, C to sides of A1 B1 C1), Tarry’s Point ``Tr = X(98)`` (antipode of St on circumcircle), and de Longchamps’ Point ``Lo = X(20)`` (reflection of H across O).

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated BrocardLemoineResult with full exact symbolic report.

geometor.casey.verify_brocard_identities_symbolic() → dict[str, sympy.Expr]¶

Verify Casey’s universal algebraic identities for cot(omega) and K’s minimum property.

  1. By the Law of Cosines, cot A + cot B + cot C = (b^2+c^2-a^2)/(4*Delta) + (c^2+a^2-b^2)/(4*Delta) + (a^2+b^2-c^2)/(4*Delta) = (a^2+b^2+c^2)/(4*Delta).

  2. By Lagrange’s identity (Sec. I, Prop. 7, p. 171): (a^2+b^2+c^2)*(x^2+y^2+z^2) - (a*x+b*y+c*z)^2 = (b*z - c*y)^2 + (c*x - a*z)^2 + (a*y - b*x)^2, proving that under a*x+b*y+c*z = 2*Delta, x^2 + y^2 + z^2 attains its unique minimum when x/a = y/b = z/c (at K = X(6)).

Returns:

Dictionary of zero-verified symbolic residuals.

geometor.casey.construct_radical_axis_and_coaxial_pencil(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → CoaxialPencilResult¶

Construct the radical axis, coaxal system, orthogonal pencil, and limiting points.

Uses exact integer-rational circles C1 = ((-10, 0), 6) and C2 = ((17, 0), 15):

  1. Draws an auxiliary secant circle C_aux = ((2, 8), 10) cutting C1 in U1, V1 and C2 in U2, V2 (Casey Book III, Prop. 28).

  2. Intersects common chords [U1 V1] and [U2 V2] to obtain the Radical Centre Rc = (0, -6) (Book III, Prop. 23, Cor. 2).

  3. Drops the perpendicular from Rc to the line of centres [O1 O2] to construct the Radical Axis x = 0 and foot D = (0, 0).

  4. Constructs the tangent D T1 to C1 by Euclid III.17 (Thales circle on O1 D) and transfers |D T1| = 8 onto [O1 O2] via circle (D, T1) to construct Poncelet’s Limiting Points L1 = (-8, 0) and L2 = (8, 0).

  5. Constructs two members (Rc, L1) and (D, T1) of the conjugate orthogonal pencil passing through L1, L2 and cutting C1, C2, C3 orthogonally.

Parameters:

model – Optional existing Model.

Returns:

Populated CoaxialPencilResult with exact coordinates and verification report.

geometor.casey.prove_casey_feuerbach_heronian() → CaseyFeuerbachHeronianReport¶

Verify Casey’s bitangent equation and all 4 Feuerbach tangencies on Heronian (15, 13, 14).

Evaluates exact coordinates on A=(0,0), B=(14,0), C=(5,12) (a=15, b=13, c=14), confirming:

  • Midpoint chords t_23 = 15/2, t_31 = 13/2, t_12 = 7.

  • Midpoint-to-incircle bitangents |Ma Ta| = 1/2, |Mb Tb| = 1/2, |Mc Tc| = 1.

  • Casey’s equation (15/2)*(1/2) + (13/2)*(1/2) - 7*1 = 15/4 + 13/4 - 7 = 0.

  • Internal tangency of Nine-Points Circle N=(6, 63/16), R_9=65/16 with Incircle I=(6, 4), r=4 at Fe = X(11) = (6, 8) with |NI| = 1/16 = R_9 - r.

  • External tangency of Nine-Points Circle with all three Excircles (Ia, r_a), (Ib, r_b), (Ic, r_c) with |N I_k| = R_9 + r_k.

  • Casey’s 1861 inversion circle (Ma, 1/2) cuts both (I, 4) and (Ia, 14) orthogonally and inverts their fourth common internal tangent into the Nine-Points Circle.

Returns:

Frozen CaseyFeuerbachHeronianReport with exact rational values.

geometor.casey.prove_casey_theorem_symbolic() → CaseySymbolicProof¶

Prove Casey’s Generalized Ptolemy Theorem & Feuerbach’s Corollary symbolically.

Verifies four algebraic pillars from Casey, Book VI, Sec. IV, Props. 9–10 (pp. 102–106):

  1. Inversion Invariance (Prop. 9, pp. 102–103): Under inversion in (O, k) at the origin, for two circles (x1, y1, r1) and (x2, y2, r2) not passing through O, the normalized bitangent ratio t_12^2 / (r1 * r2) is strictly invariant: t'_12^2 / (r'_1 * r'_2) - t_12^2 / (r1 * r2) == 0.

  2. Collinear Contact Reduction (Prop. 10, pp. 103–104): Inverting from a point on the fifth tangent circle maps it to y = 0 and the four tangent circles to (u_i, r_i) with t_ij = |u_i - u_j|, reducing t_12*t_34 + t_14*t_23 = t_13*t_24 for ordered contacts u_1 < u_2 < u_3 < u_4 to Euler’s identity (Book II, Prop. 7, p. 24).

  3. Universal Incircle Bitangent Identity (Prop. 10, Cor. 2, pp. 104–105): For general positive side lengths b <= c <= a, t_23 = a/2, t_31 = b/2, t_12 = c/2 and t_14 = (c - b)/2, t_24 = (a - c)/2, t_34 = (a - b)/2 satisfy t_23*t_14 + t_31*t_24 - t_12*t_34 == 0, alongside Casey’s squared identity (b^2 - c^2) + (c^2 - a^2) + (a^2 - b^2) == 0.

  4. Universal Excircle Bitangent Identity: For the A-excircle, t_14 = (c - b)/2, t_24 = (a + c)/2, t_34 = (a + b)/2 satisfy t_23*t_14 + t_31*t_24 - t_12*t_34 == 0 (with t_31*t_24 = t_12*t_34 + t_23*t_14).

Returns:

Frozen CaseySymbolicProof certificate with zero residuals.

geometor.casey.construct_pole_and_polar(model: geometor.model.Model | None = None) → PolePolarResult¶

Construct the Polar of D by straightedge and verify La Hire’s Reciprocity (Unit 4).

On circle (O, R0) with O = (0, 0) and R0 = (5, 0) (x^2 + y^2 = 25):

  1. Draws two rational secants cutting the circle in G1=(0, 5), H1=(4, 3) and G2=(-3, -4), H2=(5, 0), meeting at exterior pole D = (15/2, 5/4).

  2. Constructs the remaining diagonal vertices U = [G1 H2] ∩ [G2 H1] = (3, 2) and V = [G1 G2] ∩ [H1 H2] = (5/3, 10) of the inscribed quadrangle; their join [U V] is polar(D): 6x + y - 20 = 0, and D U V is a Self-Conjugate Triangle (Casey, Book VI, Sec. VII, Prop. 2, p. 142).

  3. Intersects polar(D) with both secants at J1, J2 and verifies Casey’s Harmonic Secant Theorem (G1, H1; J1, D) = (G2, H2; J2, D) = -1 (Sec. III, Prop. 4, p. 89).

  4. Selects exterior point Q = (4, -4) on polar(D), constructs its two tangents to (O, 5) by Thales semicircle on OQ, draws chord of contact polar(Q) (Book III, Prop. 27, p. 47), and asserts La Hire’s Reciprocity D in polar(Q) (Book III, Prop. 25, p. 45).

model – Optional existing Model.
