Book VI, Section III Notes: From Pappus Book VII to Chasles¶

Historical Provenance & Cross-Canon Lineage

f. 0113 · p. 87

1. Ancient Roots in Pappus Book VII (geometor/pappus)¶

The harmonic properties of collinear segments, pencils, and the complete quadrilateral descend directly from Pappus of Alexandria's Synagoge Book VII (c. 340 CE, modeled symbolically in geometor/pappus):

  • Harmonic Cross-Ratio Invariance: Synagoge VII, Prop. 129 proves that any transversal cutting a pencil of four concurrent lines with harmonic ratio is cut in a harmonic range.

  • Complete Quadrilateral Diagonals: Synagoge VII, Prop. 131 contains the foundational theorem that each diagonal of a complete quadrilateral is divided harmonically by the other two diagonals.

2. Concordance with Altshiller-Court¶

This section corresponds to College Geometry Chapter VII (Harmonic Division & Complete Quadrilaterals, §§ 345–357): * § 345: Harmonic conjugate points; * § 348: Relation to orthogonal circles ($OC \cdot OD = R^2$); * § 352: Harmonic pencils and bisected parallels; * § 355: Harmonic properties of the complete quadrilateral.
