2. sympy.geometry.Circle Capability Audit vs. geometor.casey¶

Inspection of sympy.geometry.curve.Circle in SymPy confirms that standard sympy.geometry provides basic Euclidean primitives (center, radius, equation, tangent_lines, is_tangent) yet omits the entire modern Euclidean circle-algebra toolkit codified by Casey. geometor.casey supplies exact closed-form symbolic implementations for every gap (audited programmatically via audit_sympy_circle_gaps()):
