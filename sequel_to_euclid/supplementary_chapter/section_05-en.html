SECTION V.¶

APPLICATIONS — BROCARD’S CIRCLE, BROCARD’S FIRST TRIANGLE & FEUERBACH’S THEOREM.¶

Section V, Proposition 1 (:term:`The Seven-Point Brocard Circle <brocard-circle>` & Brocard’s First Triangle). Construct the circle (Ml O) having the segment joining the Circumcentre O and Symmedian Point K as diameter (centered at Ml $= X(182)$ of radius $\frac{1}{2}|OK| = \frac{1}{2} R \sqrt{1 - 3\tan^2\omega}$*). Let the pairs of Brocard rays* [B Om1] and [C Om2] meet at A1, [C Om1] and [A Om2] at B1, and [A Om1] and [B Om2] at C1, forming Brocard’s First Triangle △A1B1C1. Then:

  1. All seven points O, K, Ω, Ω′, A1, B1, C1 lie concyclically upon the Brocard Circle (Ml O);

  2. Ω and Ω′ are symmetric across the diameter [O K] with $\angle \Omega O K = \angle \Omega' O K = \omega$ and $\angle O \Omega K = \angle O \Omega' K = 90^\circ$;

  3. Brocard’s First Triangle △A1B1C1 is inversely similar to △ABC with similarity ratio $\sin \omega$ and shares the same Centroid $G = X(2)$ as $\triangle ABC$!

f. 0221 · p. 195

Brocard’s First Triangle is inversely similar to $ABC$.

Dem.—Since $OA'$ is perpendicular to $BC$, and $OB'$ to $AC$, the angle $A'OB'$ is equal to $ACB$; but (Euclid III.21) $A'OB'$ is equal to $A'C'B'$. Hence $\triangle A'B'C'$ is inversely similar to $\triangle ABC$, and Steiner's point St ($X(99)$) and Tarry's point Tr ($X(98)$) arise as the antipodal perspective pair on the circumcircle (O A) associated with $\triangle A_1 B_1 C_1$.

f. 0227 · p. 201

Section V, Application to the Nine-Points Circle & :person:`Feuerbach’s <feuerbach>` Theorem (ff. ``0227–0229``, pp. 201–203). Taking the three similar triangles $AB'C'$, $A'BC'$, $A'B'C$ formed inside $\triangle ABC$ by its medial triangle $\triangle A'B'C'$ as three directly similar figures of Section IV, their three pairwise centres of similitude are the side midpoints $A', B', C'$, their three invariable points are the midpoints $A'', B'', C''$ of $AH, BH, CH$, and the three altitude feet $A''', B''', C'''$ are the concurrences of three triads of homologous perpendiculars—proving that the Circle of Similitude of $AB'C', A'BC', A'B'C$ IS the Nine-Points Circle of $\triangle ABC$!

f. 0228 · p. 202
Proposition 5° (Similar-Figures Proof of Feuerbach’s Theorem, pp. 201–203). The lines joining the points $A'', B'', C''$ to the centres of the inscribed circles of the triangles $AB'C', A'BC', A'B'C$ pass through the point of contact $F$ of the nine-points circle of $ABC$ with its inscribed circle.
f. 0229 · p. 203

Dem. (Conclusion).—Drawing $IF \parallel EA''$, the similarity ratio $r : R :: II' : DI$ fixes $IF = r$; because $EA''$ and $IF$ are parallel radii of the nine-points circle and the incircle of $\triangle ABC$, line $FA''$ passes through their internal centre of similitude $F$, proving tangency at $F$ (and likewise external tangency with every excircle whose centre $J'$ maps through the corresponding excentral rays). Q.E.D.
