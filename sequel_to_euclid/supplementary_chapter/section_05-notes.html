Supplementary Chapter, Section V Notes: Brocard, Neuberg, M’Cay & Feuerbach¶

Historical Provenance & Cross-Canon Concordance (etc, altshiller-court)

f. 0220 · p. 194

1. Brocard’s Circle in Algiers & Rouen (1876–1883)¶

On f. 0221 (p. 195), Casey explicitly credits Captain H. Brocard (Chef de Bataillon), noting his papers in Nouvelle Correspondance Mathématique, tomes III–VI (1876–1879), and his addresses before the Association Française pour l’Avancement des Sciences at the Algiers (1881, AFAS Alger, 1881) and Rouen (1883) Congresses, subsequently expanded by Neuberg, M’Cay, and Tucker.

f. 0228 · p. 202

2. Multiple Independent Proofs of Feuerbach’s Theorem in Casey’s Sequel¶

Together with the two inversion/Casey-theorem proofs in Book VI, Section IV (ff. 0130–0132), Section V (ff. 0227–0229) supplies a completely independent proof of Feuerbach's Nine-Points Tangency Theorem from the Circle of Similitude of the three half-scale triangles $AB'C', A'BC', A'B'C$ (further enriched by W. J. M'Clelland acknowledged in Casey’s Preface, f. 0008).
