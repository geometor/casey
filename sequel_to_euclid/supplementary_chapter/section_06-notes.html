Supplementary Chapter, Section VI Notes: Neuberg (1885) & Casey (1886)¶

Casey’s Original 1886 Royal Irish Academy Memoir in Textbook Form

f. 0231 · p. 205

1. From Neuberg’s Harmonic Quadrilaterals to Casey’s $n$-Gons¶

After Joseph Neuberg introduced harmonic quadrilaterals ($AB\cdot CD = BC\cdot DA$) in Mathesis 5 (1885), John Casey immediately generalized the entire Lemoine–Brocard–Tucker apparatus to arbitrary cyclic Harmonic $n$-gons in his landmark memoir On the Harmonic Section of Polygons (Proceedings of the Royal Irish Academy, 1886).

f. 0236 · p. 210

Casey incorporated his Academy memoir directly into Section VI (ff. 0231–0248, pp. 205–222) of the 1886 and 1888 editions, creating one of the earliest English textbook expositions bridging projective involution on a circle with classical polygon geometry (see also Altshiller-Court and geometor/pappus).
