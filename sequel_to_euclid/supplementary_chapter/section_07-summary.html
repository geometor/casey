Supplementary Chapter, Section VII Summary: Associated Figures & Tarry’s Polygons¶

Mathematical Synthesis of Casey’s Supplementary Chapter, Section VII (pp. 223–230)

f. 0249 · p. 223

1. Generalized $n$-Figure Similitude Synthesis¶

Section VII abstracts Section IV (three similar figures) and Section VI (harmonic polygons) to an arbitrary $n$-tuple of directly similar plane figures pivoted on $n$ invariable points $I_1, \dots, I_n$ concyclic on the common Circle of Similitude $Z$:

  • Perspective Theorem (Props. 2–4): Every homologous $n$-tuple of points is in perspective with $(I_1, \dots, I_n)$, and every $n$-gon formed by homologous lines admits a perspective centre $K$ whose rays to its $\binom{n}{2}$ vertices pass through the pairwise centres of similitude on $Z$.

  • Tarry’s Line & Polygon (Def. V, Props. 6–8): When homologous segments chain end-to-end as $A_1 A_2 \dots A_{n+1}$, equangularity ($\omega = \omega'$) forces closure into a cyclic harmonic polygon.

f. 0255 · p. 229

2. Exercises 1–30 Concordance¶

In ff. 0254–0256 (pp. 228–230), Casey connects associated systems of three figures $F_1, F_2, F_3$ directly to Pascal/Brianchon polarity, involution pencils of four centres of similitude, the Brocard circle, and homographic circle chords admitting a Lemoine point and two Brocard points.
