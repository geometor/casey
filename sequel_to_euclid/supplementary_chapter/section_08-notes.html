Supplementary Chapter, Section VIII Notes: Neuberg, M’Cay & de Longchamps¶

Historical Provenance & Folio Colophon (ff. 0257–0274, pp. 231–248)

f. 0257 · p. 231

1. Continental & Irish Collaborators Across Exercises 1–145¶

Across the 158 exercises of Section VIII, Casey draws extensively from Joseph Neuberg (on Neuberg circles and co-symmedian triangles), Prof. W. S. M’Cay (co-symmedian perspective lines, Ex. 13), Émile Lemoine, and Gaston Tarry, bridging Irish, British, French, and Belgian periodicals (Mathesis, Messenger of Mathematics, Educational Times) into a unified problem set.

f. 0273 · p. 247

2. Gaston Albert Gohierre de Longchamps (Exs. 146–158)¶

On f. 0273 (p. 247), Casey acknowledges M. de Longchamps (Journal de Mathématiques Spéciales, 1886; note the printed misprint “1866” on line 4 of p. 247) for the orthogonal-median-circle theorem defining $L = 2O - H = X(20)$, concluding the 1888 Dublin Fifth Edition on f. 0274 (p. 248), followed only by the publisher’s endpapers.
