Supplementary Chapter, Section VIII Summary: Neuberg’s & de Longchamps’ Circles¶

Mathematical Synthesis of Casey’s Concluding Miscellaneous Exercises (Exs. 1–158, pp. 231–248)

f. 0257 · p. 231

1. Synthesis of Exercises 1–158¶

Section VIII crowns the Sequel to Euclid with 158 research exercises spanning modern triangle geometry:

  • Neuberg’s Circle (Exs. 5–8, ff. ``0257–0258``): Fixed base $BC$ and fixed Brocard angle $\omega$ confine vertex $A$ to Neuberg’s Circle, coaxial with the midpoint $I$ of $BC$ and a line parallel to $BC$, subtending $2\omega$ at $BC$.

  • Steiner $X(99)$ & Tarry $X(98)$ Antipodal Pair: Verified in geometor.casey.brocard.construct_brocard_and_lemoine_circles() on the Heronian $(15, 13, 14)$ triangle as $S_t = \left(\frac{17253}{2353}, -\frac{9396}{2353}\right)$ and $T_r = \left(\frac{15689}{2353}, \frac{115233}{9412}\right)$ with $\frac{1}{2}(S_t + T_r) = O$.

f. 0273 · p. 247

2. de Longchamps Point $X(20)$ & Euler-Line Ledger (geometor.casey.brocard)¶

Exercises 146–158 (ff. 0273–0274) characterize de Longchamps’ Circle centered at the reflection of $H$ across $O$:

  • Symbolic Formula: $L = 2O - H = X(20)$, completing the five-point Euler line axis alongside $O = X(3)$, $G = X(2)$, $N = X(5)$, and $H = X(4)$.

  • Heronian Verification: On $A(11, 12)$, $B(0, 0)$, $C(14, 0)$, construct_brocard_and_lemoine_circles() verifies $L = \left(9, \frac{9}{2}\right)$ exactly over $\mathbb{Q}$.
